DigiVote

An independent voter survey from Parliament to Gram Panchayats—helping citizens identify capable leaders and reduce the role of money and power in elections.

Not the Election Commission of India DigiVote is a private community tool for informal sentiment only. It is not a government service and has no legal effect on any real election. For adults 18+.

What you can do

  • Sign in with an Indian (+91) mobile OTP
  • Register a simple profile (name, gender, age group, constituencies)
  • Nominate when nomination is open
  • Cast one anonymous ballot when the ballot stage is open
  • View results when they are published
  • Use pulse results for leader identification and enablement in your constituency
  • DigiVote’s digital in-app surveys run at least two months before elections—covering 543 Parliament, 4,123 Assembly, 71,881 Urban Ward, and 257,359 Gram Panchayat seats across India

Privacy & trust

  • We record that you voted — never who you voted for
  • Location is used only to suggest your constituency — GPS is never stored
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, and we do not sell your data
  • Delete your account anytime in the app
